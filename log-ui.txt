### Bug 详细说明

在 `_clusterColors` 函数中，当两张视觉上相近的颜色被合并时，加权平均公式计算错误：

**修复前**：`(avg_merged * count_merged + avg_new) / (count_merged + count_new)`
- 例：merged avg=100, count=10; new avg=200, count=20 → `(100*10 + 200) / 30 = 40` ❌

**修复后**：`(avg_merged * count_merged + avg_new * count_new) / (count_merged + count_new)`
- 例：`(100*10 + 200*20) / 30 = 167` ✅
